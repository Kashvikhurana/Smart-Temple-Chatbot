Banke Bihari Mandir

About the Temple

Banke Bihari Temple, located in the sacred town of Vrindavan, Uttar Pradesh, is one of the most revered temples dedicated to Lord Krishna. Known for its unique rituals, vibrant festivals, and spiritual ambiance, the temple attracts millions of devotees every year. The deity, Banke Bihari Ji, is a child-like manifestation of Krishna, exuding joy and divine charm.
